APPLICATION QUESTIONS

Clarify the Conditions Before Selection.

Can we discuss a project before the interface drawing is complete?

Yes. Share the intended user interaction, available space and known cleaning and host requirements. We help clarify the open decisions before drawing work is agreed.

Does this page mean the assembly is certified as a medical device?

No. This is component and assembly design support. Equipment-level safety, regulatory compliance and clinical suitability require the responsible manufacturer’s assessment and validation.

Can you guarantee compatibility with our disinfectant?

Compatibility must be reviewed for the actual material, cleaner, concentration, contact time and cleaning method. Any evaluation scope and acceptance criteria are agreed before testing.

All equipment-level safety, regulatory and clinical decisions remain with the responsible equipment team. No unverified certification or performance claim is made in this prototype.
