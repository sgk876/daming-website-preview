Typical constraints

Confirm these before a route is chosen.

Cleaner name, concentration, contact time, and method have to be specified before any material comment. This page does not state that a cover survives a named disinfectant.

  • Electrical. Host interface, supply, and connectors. Pending until the equipment team names them.
  • Mechanical. Cover outline, window, enclosure fit, and mounting.
  • Environment. Named cleaners and the cleaning method. Compatibility is pending confirmation for the actual material and process.
  • Glove and wet use. Glove type, and whether the surface is wet during use. Sample checks are agreed; they are not a blanket guarantee.
  • Quantity band. Prototype, or an annual band. There is no public price.

Questions

Before a configuration is named.

Can we talk before the interface drawing is finished?

Yes. Share the user actions, the space, and the cleaning and host facts you already have. Open items stay open.

Does this page mean the assembly is a certified medical device?

No. Equipment-level safety, regulatory compliance, and clinical suitability stay with the responsible manufacturer.

Can you guarantee compatibility with our disinfectant?

No. Cleaner, concentration, contact time, and method have to be named, and any evaluation is agreed before it is treated as a result.
