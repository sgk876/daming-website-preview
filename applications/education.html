EXAMPLE SCENARIO · NOT A CASE STUDY

Education interfaces,
as a working framework.

Classroom fronts, training stations and lab instruments can start from the same brief as any other OEM panel: who uses it, what it must show, and how it fits the enclosure. This page lists example scenarios. It does not publish a confirmed education installation.

Priority work on this site remains industrial equipment and medical devices. Education is a framework for a future project.

EXAMPLE SCENARIOS

Questions we would open, not results we claim.

EXAMPLE 01

A classroom or training front

Glare, viewing distance, mounting and how often the surface is cleaned. The cover and the touch stack are reviewed together.

EXAMPLE 02

A lab or bench instrument

Window size, tail exit, host connection and whether a stylus or a glove is part of the real use.

EXAMPLE 03

A shared station

Duty cycle, brightness and the difference between a fixed keypad and an on-screen menu. The choice follows the function list.

These are example scenarios for discussion. They are not customer names, photographs of installed equipment, or measured outcomes.
