Terminals and robot devices

Touch and display for three device classes.

DaMing reviews OEM touch and display integration for smart terminal devices, AI terminal devices, and robot devices that need a touchscreen or display. This page lists constraints to confirm and routes to enter. It is not a case study.

No customer is named. No recognition rate, brightness, or vibration rating is stated. Missing facts stay pending confirmation.

Typical constraints

Three device classes, one rule: do not fill a blank.

The classes below are entry points for a brief. They are not a claim that a catalog module is approved for that device.

Smart terminal devices

  • Mechanical. Handheld, counter, wall, or another mounting. Outline and window are pending until the equipment is named.
  • Electrical. Host, supply, and touch or video interface. Pending confirmation.
  • Environment. Indoor public use, outdoor exposure, or cleaning. Pending confirmation.
  • Glove and wet use. Only if the terminal is actually used that way.
  • Quantity band. Prototype, or an annual band.

AI terminal devices

  • Interaction. On-screen touch, fixed keys, or both. Not chosen on this page.
  • Mechanical. Openings for a camera, speaker, or sensor are cover questions only. No model performance is stated.
  • Electrical. Host video and touch interface. Pending confirmation.
  • Environment. Heat and space around the stack. Pending confirmation. No thermal rating is published.
  • Glove and wet use. If the terminal is used in the field, name the glove or the moisture.

Robot devices

  • Which surface. Teach pendant, a screen on the robot, or a separate terminal.
  • Mechanical. Mounting, cable flex, and tail routing. Shock and vibration are pending confirmation. No vibration rating is published.
  • Electrical. The controller connection and the video or touch interface. Pending until named.
  • Glove and wet use. Glove use in the work cell, if any.
  • Quantity band. Prototype, or an annual band.
