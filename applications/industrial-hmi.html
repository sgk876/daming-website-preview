APPLICATION QUESTIONS

Clarify the Conditions Before Selection.

Can we start with an enclosure and no touch drawing?

Yes. Share the available space, window, mounting and operator requirements. We identify what is fixed, compare suitable configurations and agree the drawing work needed.

Can you guarantee EMC or glove performance?

Performance targets depend on the complete equipment and defined operating conditions. We agree the relevant component configuration and sample checks; equipment-level compliance is not implied.

Can the display kit include HDMI and matching cables?

Those accessories can be reviewed within the touch display assembly scope. Panel compatibility, driver configuration, power and connections need confirmation for the project.

Component options, operating targets and validation depend on the agreed configuration. This prototype does not promise equipment-level certification or universal performance.
