Typical constraints

Confirm these before a route is chosen.

These are questions for the project. A missing answer is pending confirmation. This page does not publish a temperature limit, an IP rating, or a performance result.

  • Electrical. Host interface, supply, and controller, if one is already chosen. Pending until the equipment team names them.
  • Mechanical. Bezel space, viewing window, mounting, and where the tail or cable must exit.
  • Environment. Temperature, cleaning agents, and any washdown or outdoor exposure. Pending confirmation per project.
  • Glove and wet use. Whether operators wear gloves, and which glove. Surface moisture and ingress protection are separate questions.
  • Quantity band. Prototype, or an annual band. There is no public price and no minimum order on this page.

Questions

Before a configuration is named.

Can we start from an enclosure and no touch drawing?

Yes. Share the space, window, mounting, and operator actions. Open items stay open. The drawing work is agreed after that.

Can you guarantee EMC or glove performance?

No. Targets depend on the whole machine and the conditions you name. Sample checks are agreed for the configuration. Equipment-level compliance is not implied.

Glove and wet-touch note →
Can the display kit include a driver board and cables?

Those parts can be reviewed on the assembly route. Compatibility, power, and connectors are confirmed for the project. They are not assumed from this page.
