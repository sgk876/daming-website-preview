Typical constraints

Confirm these before a route is chosen.

These are questions for the project. A missing answer is pending confirmation. No temperature rating and no water-ingress rating is published here.

  • Electrical. The control-board connection, supply, and whether a display window needs a host. Pending until named.
  • Mechanical. Outline, windows, icon positions, and mounting on the appliance.
  • Environment. Heat near the control, cleaning, and where moisture is expected. Pending confirmation per project.
  • Glove and wet use. Wet hands, droplets, or a glove. Touch with water on the surface is not the same as sealing the appliance.
  • Quantity band. Prototype, or an annual band. There is no public price.

Questions

Before a configuration is named.

Do we choose keys or a screen first?

No. Start from the actions and the status the user must see. Fixed keys, an on-screen menu, or both can stay open until that list is clear.

Can the discussion include icons and windows?

Yes. Layout, graphics, windows, and touch construction can be reviewed together. Drawing responsibility is agreed on the project.

Does the touch panel include appliance safety control?

No. The interface scope and the appliance’s safety functions are defined separately. This page does not claim appliance approval.
