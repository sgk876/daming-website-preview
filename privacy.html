PRIVACY / INQUIRY DELIVERY

How Your Inquiry Is Handled

DaMing Touch&Display uses your name, company, email address and project brief to respond to your inquiry and discuss the requested project. The form sends these details to sgk@touch-displays.com through FormSubmit.

Before you send

Review the information and consent at the final step. Please do not include passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or confidential customer drawings. You can contact us directly instead of using the form.

Delivery provider

FormSubmit processes the submission to deliver the email. Its documentation states that submission archives are retained for 30 days. Review FormSubmit privacy terms and delivery documentation. GitHub Pages hosts this preview and may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

Delivery status

A successful form-service response means the request was accepted, not that the recipient inbox has confirmed delivery. First-time activation, spam filtering or mail delivery issues may affect receipt. Please email us directly if needed.

Contact and requests

For questions about an inquiry, correction or deletion requests, contact Si Guokun at sgk@touch-displays.com or +86 186 8872 9980.
