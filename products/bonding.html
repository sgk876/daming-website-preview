ENGINEERING QUESTIONS

Before You Finalize the Design

Which information should I prepare before requesting a recommendation?

Share your equipment type, required functions, approximate space and any fixed connections or components. Unknown details can stay open for discussion. Final suitability, development scope and sample checks are agreed for the project.

Optical bonding review checklist →
Can the customer supply the LCD?

We can evaluate a customer-supplied LCD as part of the project. Model, condition, specification, samples and material responsibilities need to be agreed before work begins.

Is optical bonding always the right choice?

No. The decision depends on the optical goals, construction, component suitability and project constraints. We discuss the trade-offs and alternatives before confirming the approach.

Can you promise a particular optical or reliability result?

Targets and test conditions are agreed for the actual components and assembly. A representative sample is used for validation before production decisions.
