ENGINEERING QUESTIONS

Before You Finalize the Design

Which information should I prepare before requesting a recommendation?

Share your equipment type, required functions, approximate space and any fixed connections or components. Unknown details can stay open for discussion. Final suitability, development scope and sample checks are agreed for the project.

Compare glass, PC and PMMA →
Which is best: glass, PC or PMMA?

There is no single best choice for every panel. We compare material grades and construction against appearance, installation, cleaning, handling and exposure requirements.

Can you help if we only have a product photo?

A photo can help explain the appearance. We then clarify dimensions, windows, mounting and material requirements before developing the drawing.

Is this a complete touch panel?

This category covers the cover panel itself. If touch sensing is also required, we review it with the PCAP or capacitive keypad solution and confirm the combined scope.
