SOLUTION OVERVIEW

What is a custom PCAP touch panel?

A projected capacitive (PCAP) touch panel detects touch through a sensor connected to a controller. A custom project defines the cover, sensor outline, FPC routing, interface and operating requirements together.

ENGINEERING QUESTIONS

Before You Finalize the Design

Which information should I prepare before requesting a recommendation?

Share your equipment type, required functions, approximate space and any fixed connections or components. Unknown details can stay open for discussion. Final suitability, development scope and sample checks are agreed for the project.

PCAP design input checklist →
Do we need a finished drawing to start?

No. Application information, approximate space and a reference image can be enough for an initial discussion. We identify the dimensions and interfaces needed before drawing work is agreed.

Can we customize the cover while reviewing the touch configuration?

Yes. We review the cover and touch construction together. Material, thickness, printing, sensor and controller choices need to suit the complete assembly.

Can you guarantee operation with water or gloves?

Performance must be evaluated for the defined glove, moisture, cover, controller and equipment conditions. We agree on those conditions and the prototype checks for the project.
