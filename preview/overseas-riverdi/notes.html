Knowledge

What we will say before a drawing exists.

Short notes for sourcing and engineering conversations. They are not testimonials, white papers, or a guarantee of glove, water or lifetime performance.

Engineering reply

The reply has three parts.

FIXED

What you already decided

Size, window, host, material, or a reference sample you want to stay close to.

OPEN

What can wait

Print, tail length, controller, bonding and cables stay open until they affect one another.

CHECK

What the sample must show

The questions the first piece has to answer, written down before anyone treats a photo as a part number.

The current preview states a 4-hour engineering reply target. Treat it as a target. If a form submission is your first message to the inbox, the recipient may still need to activate the form service.

Glove and wet touch

Two different requirements.

Tell us the glove material and thickness, the moisture, and the behavior you expect. Sensor, cover and controller are reviewed together. Sample checks are agreed for that stack.

ON THE GLASS

Water while touching

Tuning and cover thickness change the result. It is not assumed from a standard product line.

AT THE SEAL

Ingress protection

Keeping water out of the equipment is a mechanical requirement. It is not the same test as wet-finger tuning.

NOT A PROMISE

No blanket rating

This preview does not publish an IP rating, a glove thickness limit, or a pass rate.
