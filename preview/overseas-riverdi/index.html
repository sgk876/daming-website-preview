Before the design is frozen

Questions we can answer plainly.

Can a project start without drawings?

Yes. Describe the equipment, the intended functions and the space you have. A sketch or a reference product helps. Finished drawings are not required to begin. The drawing work is agreed after the requirements are clear.

Where should we start: keypad, PCAP or a full assembly?

Start with how people operate the equipment. Fixed functions can be touch keys. On-screen interaction may call for a PCAP panel. A display adds the screen and the connection. We compare those routes against the project. Nothing on this site is a quotation.

Do you publish a price or a minimum order?

No. There is no cart and no public price. Quantity, development cost and schedule are agreed for that project. A one-year warranty was described on a previous site; the term that applies is the one in the quotation.

Can touch work with gloves or water?

Share the glove material and thickness, the moisture, and the behavior you expect. Sensor, cover and controller are reviewed together, then sample checks are agreed. Water on the surface and protection against water ingress are different requirements. Neither is a blanket guarantee.
