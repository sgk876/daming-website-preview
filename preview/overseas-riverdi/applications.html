Requirement sets

Where the front has to fit the machine.

Each block lists constraints to bring into a review. None of them is a case study. No customer logo, no export list, and no measured field result is published here.

01 · Industrial equipment

Industrial HMI

Operator panels and embedded machine displays. Bring the mechanics, the host interface and the conditions on the line. Unknown items stay pending. Finished drawings are not required to start.

Fit

Mechanics

Window, outline, thickness and how the front mounts in the bezel.

Host

Interface

Touch connection, video connection, power and cable exit.

Field

Conditions

Gloves, moisture, temperature and what the sample must demonstrate.

Open

Not claimed

No customer is named. No uptime or brightness result is stated.

Start an HMI brief

02 · Controls

HVAC and water heating

Use this when you know the functions of a water heater, heat pump or HVAC controller and still need a front-panel route. The touch interface is not the appliance safety control.

KEYS

Which actions are fixed

Power, set-points, fan, timer and any window that still needs a display.

COVER

How the face is built

Material, print, icons and the openings the enclosure needs.

CHECK

What the sample represents

Moisture questions are defined. They are not assumed from a concept drawing.

Start a controls brief

03 · Medical equipment

Interfaces, as a requirement set.

This is component and assembly design support. It is not a device certification and not a case study. No biocompatibility, disinfectant resistance or clinical suitability is claimed.

CLEAN

What touches the surface

Cleaning agents and gloves, named so the cover and tuning can be reviewed.

ROUTE

Cover, touch, display

The same stack questions as any OEM front, with the device team’s validation plan.

SCOPE

Documents, not approval

An ISO 13485 copy can be reviewed. It does not approve the finished equipment.

Start a medical-interface brief

04 · Terminals and robot devices

Three device classes, one review.

Smart terminals, AI terminals and robot devices that need a touchscreen or a display. Say which class it is, then list what is already fixed. No recognition rate, brightness or vibration rating is published.
