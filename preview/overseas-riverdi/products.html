Discuss a PCAP panel

Cover lenses

Glass, PC or PMMA, decided with the stack.

The front face is outline, window, printing, mounting and surface, reviewed together. Compare the whole construction: grade, thickness, coatings and the cleaning agents the equipment will see. Glass, PC and PMMA trade weight, impact, surface life and appearance differently. No finish on a photograph is a released specification.

Touch keypads

Fixed functions, a cover, and a connection.

Bring the button actions, the icon layout or a reference panel. We coordinate sensing, any display window, and the control-board connection. The images in this library, including water-heater style layouts, are illustrative. No customer is named and no outcome is stated.

Optical bonding

Name the layers before the process.

In-house work discussed on the current site includes FPCBA bonding, ITO sensor-related lamination, OCA lamination, cover-lens and FOG bonding, and LCD optical bonding. Frame bonding and full bonding were named for CTP plus LCM. Suitability is confirmed against the actual stack. Process range is not a guarantee for every construction.

Review a stack

LCM range

A family of modules, not a SKU list.

The previous LCD page named 0.96 inch to 10.1 inch. A factory paragraph said 0.96 to 55 inches. A PCAP paragraph said 0.96 to 32 inches. Those spans do not agree. Confirm which one applies. Modes named as a family — TN, IPS, a-Si, LTPS, IGZO, OLED, monochrome TFT and custom TFT — are not tied to a part number here. The photograph is a library LCD, not a datasheet image for a named module.

Ask about a module

Touch-display assemblies

Touch, display, and the host, matched once.

An assembly can include the LCD, the touch panel, bonding, and an optional driver board and cables. Host connection, power, connector and cable length are confirmed for the agreed configuration. The cable photograph in the library is evidence of custom wiring, not a standard harness you can order by a public code.
