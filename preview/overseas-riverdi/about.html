Project path

Define, coordinate, then sample.

01

Define

An application description, a sketch or a reference product can start the discussion. Separate what is fixed from what is still open.

02

Coordinate

Cover, touch, display, FPC and supply scope are one configuration. LCD sourcing supports the integration. Touch remains the core of the work.

03

Develop

Agreed drawings, a representative sample and named checks. Sample validation does not by itself release production.

In-house processes discussed today include FPCBA bonding, ITO sensor-related lamination, OCA lamination, cover and FOG bonding, and LCD optical bonding. Supply coordination can include covers, ITO sensors, FPCBA, die-cut materials, project-specific LCDs, and optional HDMI driver boards and cables. Sourced parts and in-house work are identified in the project scope.
