Questions

Before the design is frozen.

Can we start a custom touch project without drawings?

Yes. Describe the application, the intended functions and the approximate space. A sketch or a reference product helps. Finished drawings are not required to begin. We clarify the requirements, then agree the drawing work.

Start without drawings →
Touch keypad, PCAP panel or touch display: where should we start?

Begin with how people operate the equipment. Fixed functions can be touch keys. On-screen interaction may call for a PCAP panel. A display adds the screen and the connection. We compare those routes against the project.

How do we choose glass, PC or PMMA?

Compare the whole construction: grade, thickness, coatings, printing and mounting. Glass, PC and PMMA trade weight, impact, surface life and appearance differently. Share the cleaning agents and the exposure so compatibility can be reviewed.

Can you match an LCD, a driver board and the cables?

We review the LCD model or the required specification, the host connection, power and the installation. The driver board, firmware, connectors and cables have to match the agreed configuration.

Can touch operate with water or gloves?

Share the glove material and thickness, the moisture and the behavior you expect. Sensor, cover and controller are reviewed together, then sample checks are agreed. Touch with water on the surface and protection against water ingress are different requirements. Neither is a blanket guarantee.
