DESIGN GUIDE / OPTICAL BONDING

Prepare an Optical Bonding Project Review

Begin with the actual components and desired outcome. Method selection and validation depend on the assembly, incoming materials and project requirements.

THE QUESTION

Can my cover, touch panel and LCD be integrated?

01 / DESIGN INPUT

Identify the components and goal

List the LCD, cover and touch components already selected, their available specifications and what the assembly is intended to achieve.

  • LCD model and available specifications
  • Cover and touch models or dimensions
  • Desired optical, mechanical or integration outcome
02 / DESIGN INPUT

Compare integration routes

Discuss optical bonding or an air-gap construction against the selected parts, assembly constraints, service needs and cost objectives. Neither route is selected from the name alone.

  • Stack, alignment and thickness requirements
  • Incoming part condition and supply responsibilities
  • Integration, handling and service constraints
03 / DESIGN INPUT

Agree acceptance before processing

Define representative samples, inspection conditions, functional checks and responsibility for customer-supplied components.

  • Appearance and alignment criteria
  • Display/touch checks and validation conditions
  • Incoming material and sample review arrangements

This guide helps prepare a project discussion. It is not a product specification, engineering approval or guarantee. The actual configuration, drawing work, performance targets and validation conditions require project review.
