THE QUESTION

Glass, PC or PMMA: what should I compare?

01 / DESIGN INPUT

Define appearance and fit

Identify the required outline, windows, thickness, weight objectives, printing and installation constraints before comparing options.

  • Outline, windows, holes and mounting
  • Thickness and appearance requirements
  • Printing, color and transparency expectations
02 / DESIGN INPUT

Specify the use conditions

Provide cleaning agents and methods, handling, impact concerns and exposure conditions. Avoid assuming all grades or coatings behave alike.

  • Cleaning agent, concentration and contact conditions
  • Handling and exposure scenarios
  • Surface and appearance acceptance criteria
03 / DESIGN INPUT

Compare the proposed constructions

Review material grade, treatment, graphics and assembly compatibility as a complete configuration. Agree what must be checked on samples.

  • Glass, PC or PMMA candidate configurations
  • Treatment and printing compatibility questions
  • Dimensional and application-specific sample checks

This guide helps prepare a project discussion. It is not a product specification, engineering approval or guarantee. The actual configuration, drawing work, performance targets and validation conditions require project review.
