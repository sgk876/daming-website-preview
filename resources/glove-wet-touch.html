Operating conditions

Glove and wet-touch, defined for the sample.

Share the glove and the water. Sensor, cover and controller are reviewed together, then the sample checks are written down. Nothing on this page is an unconditional feature.

PCAP design inputs

What we need named.

Which glove?

Material, thickness, and whether the glove is wet, powdered, or coated. The previous product pages listed rubber, cotton, work and medical gloves, plus thicker or coated gloves as a customization topic. Those words describe a conversation. They are not a test report, and they are not a promise that every glove works on every stack.

Water on the surface, or water kept out?

Touch response when moisture is on the cover, and sealing against ingress, are different requirements. The previous pages described operation on a dry region when a small amount of water is present, a possible shutoff when water is heavy, and recovery after the surface is wiped. Treat that as a behavior to specify and sample, not as a shipped mode.

What is not guaranteed?

Glove performance and EMC depend on the complete equipment. We agree the configuration and the sample checks. Equipment-level compliance is not implied. IEC levels and medical EMC lines that were pasted onto the old product pages are not repeated here as DaMing results.

Where does this sit in the project?

Name the condition at evaluation, confirm it when the stack is extended, and write the check into the sample plan. A cover thickness change or a different controller can reopen the question.
