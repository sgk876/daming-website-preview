Shenzhen Daming Photoelectric Technology Co., Ltd.

Shenzhen Daming Photoelectric Technology Co., Ltd. is at Yanchuan Xingda Road No. 8, Bao’an, Shenzhen, China. The facility stated for this site is 5,500 m², with Class 100 / 1,000 cleanrooms named on the current preview and a stated monthly capacity of 1.5 million pieces. Qualifications stated for external copy are National High-tech Enterprise and Specialized & Innovative (专精特新). ISO copies further down are for review of the issuing entity, date and scope.

Touch, display and optical bonding are in-house routes, with cleanroom manufacture. The delivery scope is confirmed in the project review.

DaMing Touch&Display focuses on custom touch interfaces and touch display integration for equipment and hardware manufacturers. Our work starts with how the equipment will be used and how its interface needs to fit, connect and operate.

We bring touch-panel design discussions, cover and connection requirements, bonding and coordinated component supply into the same project conversation. LCD sourcing is a supporting integration service; touch solutions remain the core of our work.

The priority conversations are industrial equipment, including smart hardware and robotics, and medical devices. HVAC and water-heating controls remain available when that is the program in front of the team.

PROCESS

From the brief to a reviewed sample.

Seven steps, shared across touch, display and bonding. The route in scope is named at the start.

  1. 01
    Name the route. Confirm whether the work is a touch panel, a display module, optical bonding, or a combination, and what the equipment must do.
  2. 02
    Prepare the touch construction: cover, sensor, FPC and controller for the agreed outline.
  3. 03
    Prepare the display module on the display route when a screen is in scope.
  4. 04
    Bond the stack only when optical bonding is part of the agreed configuration.
  5. 05
    Match driver boards, HDMI boards and front housings to that configuration.
  6. 06
    Inspect by the categories below. The checks are written for the build.
  7. 07
    Review the sample and the drawing revision before the next stage.

TESTING

Checks grouped by category.

Equipment is described by the kind of check, not by a model list. The set used on a program is the one agreed in review.

Environment
