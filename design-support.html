01Understand Your RequirementsStart with what the equipment needs to do, not a finished specification.
YOU BRING

Describe the device, users, control functions and approximate space. A photo, sketch or reference product can help.

WE WORK THROUGH

We ask about operation, environment, installation and existing components. Confirmed facts and unknowns are recorded separately.

THE OUTPUT

A requirements brief with priorities, open questions and constraints.

Confirmation pointConfirm the intended use and what still needs to be investigated.

02Recommend & Compare OptionsSee the possible routes and understand their trade-offs.
YOU BRING

Share your priorities: appearance, fit, interaction, cost objectives, quantities and timing — where known.

WE WORK THROUGH

We compare suitable product types, cover materials, touch approaches, display configurations and integration scope.

THE OUTPUT

A recommended approach with alternatives, assumptions and questions for review.

Confirmation pointAgree which route to assess further; suitability remains subject to engineering review.

03Confirm the Component SelectionTurn a preferred route into a coordinated configuration.
YOU BRING

Provide known host connections, power, enclosure dimensions and any components already selected.

WE WORK THROUGH

We review the cover, sensor, controller, display and optional driver board and cable configuration as applicable.

THE OUTPUT

A proposed configuration, supply boundaries and the key requirements to confirm.

Confirmation pointAgree the selected configuration, responsibilities and development scope, costs and schedule.

04Develop & Review the DrawingsMake dimensions, layouts and interfaces explicit before samples.
YOU BRING

Confirm appearance and installation constraints; review the proposed dimensions and connection requirements.

WE WORK THROUGH

We develop the drawings agreed for the project and work through feedback with your team, keeping revisions identifiable.

THE OUTPUT

Agreed drawing deliverables, such as cover outline, key layout, touch/FPC outline, assembly or connection drawings.

Confirmation pointCustomer and engineering review confirms the drawing revision and sample configuration. Complete equipment design is only included if explicitly agreed.

05Build Samples & ValidateCheck the agreed design in the intended equipment context.
YOU BRING

Provide the host, enclosure, representative operating conditions or test arrangements needed for validation.

WE WORK THROUGH

We coordinate the agreed sample work and checks, document feedback and identify any changes requiring another review.

THE OUTPUT

Sample feedback, check results and a revision or confirmation record within the agreed scope.

Confirmation pointResolve open issues and agree production requirements separately. A drawing or prototype is not an automatic production release.

BEFORE WE START

Questions About Working Together

What is the minimum information needed to begin?

Tell us what the equipment is, what the user needs to do and any known space or connection constraints. Approximate information is fine for the initial discussion. We then identify what needs to be confirmed.

Can you recommend the solution instead of only quoting a drawing?

Yes. We help clarify requirements and compare product types, materials and component configurations. The recommendation is reviewed with your team before the selected design and development work are agreed.

What drawings can you help develop?

Depending on the project scope, these may include cover outlines, key layouts, touch/FPC outlines, assembly layouts or connection drawings. The required drawing types, formats and responsibilities are agreed before that work starts.

Are design work and prototypes free?

Costs depend on the agreed scope and configuration. Design deliverables, sample work, quantities and schedules are discussed for the project; this page does not promise free drawings or samples.

What if the sample needs changes?

We review feedback against the agreed requirements, identify the changes and update the relevant design records. Any further development, samples, costs and timing are confirmed as needed.

How do we move from validation to production?

Open issues, the approved configuration, production requirements and acceptance criteria need confirmation. Production readiness is a separate engineering and business decision after sample review.
