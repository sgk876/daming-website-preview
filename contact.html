BEFORE YOU CONTACT US

What Helps Us Recommend a Solution?

Can we start without drawings?

Yes. Describe the equipment, intended functions and approximate installation space. We clarify the requirements, compare options and agree on drawing work before prototype development.

See the design-support process →
What should we include in the first message?

Include the application, functions, environment, host connection if known, approximate space and estimated quantities. Identify anything still undecided. A sketch or reference photo can help.

How should we share confidential drawings?

Contact Chloe or Si Guokun directly first to agree on confidentiality and the transfer method. This inquiry form does not accept file uploads.

Can we confirm price and lead time from the website?

Pricing, minimum order quantities, development scope, sample timing and production schedules are agreed for the specific configuration. Use the project brief to explain your starting point.
